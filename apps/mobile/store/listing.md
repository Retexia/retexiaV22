## Full description (max 4000)

Retexia is the app for businesses that use Retexia. Sign in with your Retexia account and keep up with your shop from anywhere.

LINGO FEEDER
Lingo is your WhatsApp sales assistant. With the app you can:
• See every chat Lingo has with your customers, live
• Know at once when a customer wants a call, with a notification and a "Call them" list
• Call a customer with one tap, then mark them as called
• Pause Lingo in a chat and answer the customer yourself
• See new orders and share the courier file straight from your phone
• Try Lingo yourself and see how she answers customers

YOUR DAY AT A GLANCE
• Today's chats, orders and sales on the home screen
• Notifications for calls, new orders and WhatsApp number problems
• An inbox with everything you were told about

MADE FOR YOUR PHONE
• Works in light and dark mode
• Lock the app with your fingerprint or face
• Your last data stays on the phone, so you can check it even without a connection

You need a Retexia account to use this app. Your account and its products are managed on retexia.com.

Questions? Visit retexia.com.
